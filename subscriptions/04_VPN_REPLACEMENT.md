## Recommendation: Mullvad (privacy) or ProtonVPN (value + email bundle)

### Mullvad ($5/mo flat, no discounts, no accounts)
- Generate a number, pay, connect. No email, no name.
- WireGuard-based, fast on macOS
- Swedish company, multiple independent audits
- Simple and honest — exactly one plan at one price
- Best for: privacy purist, technical user

### ProtonVPN Plus ($4/mo on 2yr)
- Swiss privacy laws
- If you ever want ProtonMail for case-specific email, it bundles
- Good macOS client
- Secure Core (double-hop through privacy-friendly countries)
- Best for: if you also want to upgrade email infrastructure

## Steps to Switch

### If keeping NordVPN:
1. Log in to https://my.nordaccount.com
2. Update payment method
3. Done

### If switching to Mullvad:
1. Go to https://mullvad.net
2. Click "Generate account" — no email needed
3. Pay $5 (crypto, card, or cash-in-envelope)
4. Download macOS client
5. Import WireGuard config
6. Let NordVPN lapse naturally

### If switching to ProtonVPN:
1. Sign up at https://protonvpn.com
2. Choose Plus plan ($4/mo on 2yr)
3. Download macOS client
4. Consider also setting up ProtonMail for case-specific email routing
