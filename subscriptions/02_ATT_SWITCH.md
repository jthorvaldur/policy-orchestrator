# AT&T Wireless — Switch to MVNO

**Current cost: $74/mo ($888/yr)**
**Target cost: $25/mo ($300/yr)**
**Savings: $49/mo ($588/yr)**

## Recommended: Mint Mobile or US Mobile

### Option A: Mint Mobile (T-Mobile network)
- $25/mo for unlimited (if paid annually: $15/mo for 5GB, $20/mo 15GB, $25/mo unlimited)
- Prepaid annual = $180-300/yr vs AT&T $888/yr
- T-Mobile 5G coverage
- eSIM support for iPhone

### Option B: US Mobile (Verizon or T-Mobile, your choice)
- $25/mo unlimited on either network
- Can choose Verizon if T-Mobile coverage is poor in your area
- Flexible — switch networks if one doesn't work

### Option C: Visible (Verizon MVNO, owned by Verizon)
- $25/mo unlimited, single plan, no tiers
- Verizon network
- Simple

## Steps to Switch

### Step 1: Check Your AT&T Status
1. Confirm your AT&T account ending 5104 is not under contract
2. Check if your phone is unlocked: Settings > General > About > Carrier Lock
3. If locked, request unlock from AT&T (must be paid up and no active contract)

### Step 2: Port Your Number
1. Get your AT&T account number and PIN/passcode
2. Call AT&T: 800-331-0500 or check online at att.com/myatt
3. Do NOT cancel AT&T before porting — canceling first loses your number

### Step 3: Activate New Service
1. Order SIM/eSIM from chosen provider
2. During activation, enter AT&T account number + PIN to port
3. Porting completes in minutes to hours
4. AT&T auto-cancels once port completes

### Step 4: Verify
1. Test calls, texts, data
2. Confirm AT&T final bill and autopay is cancelled
3. Update any 2FA that uses your phone number

## Timeline
- Can be done in one afternoon
- Best to do mid-billing cycle to avoid prorated charges
